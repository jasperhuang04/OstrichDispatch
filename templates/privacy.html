YOUR PRIVATE SPACE

What OstrichDispatch keeps.

OstrichDispatch is a privately operated order tracker. It reads connected email accounts to extract retailer names, order numbers, product names, purchase totals, addresses, delivery dates and tracking numbers. It does not send email, mark messages as read, or delete messages.

Email contents are processed locally on the operator's server and are not saved as raw messages or sent to an AI service. Parsed order details and hashes used to avoid duplicates are stored in the database. Mail credentials and refresh tokens are encrypted using a server-held key.

If the operator enables EasyPost, tracking numbers and carrier names are sent to EasyPost to obtain carrier updates. If you enable Discord, order names and delivery statuses are sent to your chosen Discord channel. Browser push subscriptions use your browser's push provider; notification payloads are encrypted by the Web Push protocol.

Orders stay until you delete your account or the operator removes them. Disconnecting an inbox deletes its stored credentials and sync identifiers, but preserves existing orders. Revoke the app's access in your email provider's settings if you also want to invalidate its authorization. Deleting an account removes its application data; backups may retain older records until the operator rotates them.

Other members cannot access your data through the app. The person operating the server and its backups can access the database and encryption key. Contact that person about backup retention, access, or corrections.
